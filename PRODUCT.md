# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Active job seekers, especially professionals in India, who want to reduce the daily work of searching multiple portals. They use the app to review CV-matched opportunities, manage alerts, find hiring managers, and track outreach from a private workspace.

## Product Purpose

Job Search Agent searches job portals twice daily, scores listings against each user's CV and preferences, and delivers the strongest matches through the app and email. Success means users spend less time scanning low-fit roles and can act quickly on relevant opportunities.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, and dependable. The interface should feel focused and credible, with enough warmth to support a stressful job-search workflow without becoming playful or promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative AI gradients, glass-heavy surfaces, oversized metrics, noisy badge collections, and interfaces that make automation feel opaque. Avoid visual novelty that slows down routine review and configuration.

## Design Principles

1. Make the agent's work legible: show what runs, when it runs, and what the user will receive.
2. Put high-value matches and next actions ahead of system detail.
3. Keep destructive and infrequent controls available without letting them compete with primary tasks.
4. Use consistent, familiar product controls so users can move quickly.
5. Design empty, loading, success, and error states as part of the workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access. Preserve visible focus states, support reduced motion, avoid color-only status communication, use clear labels, and keep layouts usable on narrow mobile screens.
