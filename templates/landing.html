{# ─── How it works ─────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
How it works

Three things happen while you sleep.

  1. 01

    Scrape

    LinkedIn, Naukri, HiringCafe, Indeed, Hacker News and Remotive — pulled twice a day on a scheduled job at 07:00 and 19:00 IST. No browser tabs, no manual refreshing.

  2. 02

    Score

    Each listing is read against your actual CV and preferences. You get a match score out of 100 and the specific skills a role is asking for that your CV doesn't show yet.

  3. 03

    Reach out

    For the roles worth pursuing, it finds the hiring manager, links their profile, and drafts a cold email you can edit. You approve — nothing is sent on your behalf.

{# ─── What you get ─────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
What you get

A workspace, not another job board.

CV-scored matches

Every role is ranked against your CV out of 100, so the top of your list is genuinely the top of your list. The matches that don't fit never reach you.

Six portals, twice a day

One place for LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, HiringCafe, Hacker News and Remotive — deduplicated, so you read each role once instead of six times.

Hiring-manager contacts

For high-match roles it surfaces who's likely hiring and links their profile, so a referral or a direct note is one step away instead of a research project.

Outreach drafts you approve

A first-draft cold email written from your CV and the role. You edit and send it yourself — the agent never emails anyone without you.

A daily digest

The morning's best matches, summarised. Skim it over coffee; open the app only when something is worth a closer look.

Reminders that follow up

Track what you've applied to and get nudged to follow up at the right time, so promising threads don't go cold.

{# ─── Trust / data & privacy ───────────────────────────────────── #}
Your data & privacy

Built to be boring about your data.

This is a personal tool, not an ad business. Here's exactly what it touches and what it doesn't.

  • What's collected

    Your name and email from Google sign-in, the CV you upload, and your search preferences. That's it.

  • What's never done

    Your CV and contacts are never sold, shared, or used to train anyone's models. There are no third-party ad trackers.

  • Where it lives

    Job listings are public postings scraped from portals. Your private data sits in your own account and stays scoped to you.

  • You stay in control

    Delete your CV or your whole account whenever you want. Read the full privacy policy.
