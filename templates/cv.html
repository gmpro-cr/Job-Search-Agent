How to Get More Shortlists
Actionable steps based on your profile and market data.
1 Tailor for every role

Recruiters spend 8–15 s on a resume. Ctrl+F the job description — every keyword that appears in the JD but not in your resume is a missed ATS point. Keep 2–3 versions for Finance, AI, and SaaS roles.

2 Pass the ATS filter

Use a plain one-column PDF — no colours, tables, icons, or graphics. ATS systems can't parse them. Verify at resumeworded.com. Every bullet: what you did + how + measurable impact.

3 Impact-first bullet points

Max 4–5 bullets per role; max 2 lines each. Lead with a past-tense verb. Format: "Reduced [X] by [Y]% by [method], resulting in [business impact]." Quantify time saved, revenue, retention, or user growth.

4 Optimise LinkedIn

Headline: "AI Product Builder | Banking & Credit Risk | 9 yrs". About: PM skills + domain edge + passion. Add "User Research", "Product Roadmap", "Stakeholder Management" to skills and collect endorsements. Post once/week.

5 Cold outreach to HMs

80 % of roles are filled through referrals. Find the hiring manager on LinkedIn, send a 3-line note — why the company, one relevant achievement, clear ask. Use the Hiring Managers tab in this app to build your outreach list.

6 Build PM case studies

Create 4–6 case studies for your top target companies — problem → your thinking → proposed solution. Attach the portfolio link to your resume and LinkedIn. Product teardowns and PRDs work well for finance / AI roles.
