Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

Job Search Agent is a personal job-search workspace, not an advertising business. This page explains exactly what data it touches, why, and the control you have over it. Plain language, no dark patterns.

Who runs this

Job Search Agent is built and operated by Gaurav Mahale, based in Pune, India. For any privacy question or request, email mahalegauravk@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Google account basics. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We use Google's standard sign-in scope and do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, or contacts.
  • Your CV. The résumé file you upload, and the text extracted from it, so listings can be scored against your experience.
  • Your preferences. The roles, locations, salary range, and filters you set.
  • Activity within the app. Which listings you view, mark applied, hide, or save, and reminders you create — so the workspace stays useful to you.

What we do not collect or do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your CV, contacts, or personal data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data to train machine-learning models, ours or anyone else's.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or behavioural ad trackers.
  • We do not send emails on your behalf. Outreach drafts are written for you to review and send yourself.

How job listings are gathered

The agent reads publicly posted job listings from LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, HiringCafe, Hacker News, and Remotive on a fixed schedule (07:00 and 19:00 IST). These are public postings; your private data is never attached to them and is never sent to those portals.

How your data is used

  • To score listings against your CV and preferences and produce your daily brief.
  • To surface likely hiring-manager contacts for high-match roles from public sources.
  • To generate draft outreach text that you edit and send yourself.
  • To send you a digest or reminders, if you've enabled them.

Where your data is stored

Your account data and CV are stored in a managed Postgres database and file store hosted on Vercel and Neon. Access is scoped to your account. We rely on these providers' standard security measures and access controls.

Third-party services

We use a small set of providers strictly to run the service:

  • Google — sign-in / authentication.
  • Vercel & Neon — hosting, database, and file storage.
  • An AI model provider — to score listings and draft outreach text. Content sent for processing is not used to train their models.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your CV at any time from the Profile & CV page, and you can request deletion of your entire account and associated data by emailing mahalegauravk@gmail.com. We'll action it promptly.

You may also request a copy of the personal data held about you, or ask us to correct it. You can revoke this app's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account permissions.

Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your CV, preferences, and per-account activity are removed. Public job listings are retained in the shared database independent of any individual account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date above will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or deletion requests: mahalegauravk@gmail.com. See also the Terms of Use.
