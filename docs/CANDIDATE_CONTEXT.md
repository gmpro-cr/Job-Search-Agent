## Fit rules (flag poor fits proactively)

- Penalise: retail-mortgage / consumer-risk roles (domain mismatch), roles
  needing deep infra/ML engineering, very senior titles (Director/Head) or
  experience floors well above 9 years, relocation-only roles outside Pune
  (Delhi/abroad only if explicitly flagged as acceptable).
- Boost: lending, credit, underwriting, decisioning, collections, KYC/AML,
  conversational/voice AI in banking, LLM-applied products in BFSI.
- Watch for red flags: recruiters on personal Gmail, "no fee" warnings,
  duplicate-vendor submissions for the same role.

## Outreach / email drafting rules

- Crisp; under ~100 words where possible; full sentences; lead with the hook.
- When mentioning AI work, focus on **Vaani** and **CreditGuardAI**.
- No questions in application emails; avoid directly asking for a call (softer ask).
- Say "CV attached" — he attaches the CV himself.
- Show the full draft text, not just a Gmail link.
- Voice: concise, declarative, banking abbreviations are fine, no corporate fluff.
- Contact finding: he prefers self-built tools over paid APIs (e.g. Apollo) for
  finding emails, and **never** wants guessed addresses — only verified public ones.
